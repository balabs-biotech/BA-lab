Preamble

The CERN Open Hardware Licence P (OHL-P) is a free and open source licence to facilitate
the collaborative development of products, where the design is expressed in terms of
hardware description languages and register-transfer level (RTL) specifications.

Whereas:

(A) The evolution of society and the technological capacities of mankind render obsolete
    the claims upon which traditional intellectual-property regimes were based;

(B) Furthermore, it is obvious to anybody involved in the development of tangible goods
    that firmware and software have become inseparable from the hardware components of
    such goods and their functional properties; and

(C) When software is separate or separable from that hardware, that software as such
    remains under other licence regimes that are equally valid.

Now therefore the licensors have decided to publish this Licence so that the talents
and resources of any member of society may be applied to its projects.
