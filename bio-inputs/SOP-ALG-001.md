# 🌿 SOP-ALG-001: Thermochemical Hydrolysis of Marine Algal Biomass for Biostimulant Formulations

* **Target Platform:** MARINOR Seaweed Hydrolysate / Crop Biostimulants
* **Author:** B.A. LAB project — Algology & Bio-Inputs Unit

---

## 🎯 1. Objective & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the controlled thermochemical alkaline hydrolysis process for converting raw brown algal biomass into a concentrated, bio-available liquid hydrolysate rich in alginates, mannitol, laminarin, free amino acids, and natural phytohormones (auxins, cytokinins). The end product serves as a primary biostimulant matrix for soil drench and foliar applications.

---

## ⚠️ 2. Safety, Biosafety & PPE

* **Biosafety Grade:** BSL-1 / Chemical Hazard (Alkali handling).
* **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):**
  * Heavy-duty neoprene gloves and lab apron.
  * Full-face shield during alkali addition ($1M - 2M\ \text{KOH}$ / $\text{NaOH}$).
  * FFP2 dust mask during dry seaweed milling operations.
* **Chemical Hazards:** Concentrated potassium hydroxide ($\text{KOH}$) causes severe skin burns and eye damage. Always add alkali pellets to water, never water to pellets.

---

## 🔬 3. Equipment & Reagents

### Equipment
* Stainless Steel / Glass-Lined Stirred Reaction Vessel with thermal jacket ($90^\circ\text{C}$ capable)
* Industrial Mechanical Homogenizer / High-Shear Mixer
* High-Precision pH Meter (Two-point calibrated at pH 4.01 and 7.00)
* Stainless Steel Micro-Mesh Sieve ($50\ \mu\text{m}$) or Centrifuge

### Reagents & Inputs
* Dry Milled Algal Biomass (Particle size $< 0.5\text{mm}$)
* Potassium Hydroxide ($\text{KOH}$) analytical grade ($>90\%$ purity)
* Citric Acid / Phosphoric Acid ($\text{H}_3\text{PO}_4$) for pH neutralization
* Preservative: Potassium Sorbate ($0.2\%\text{ w/v}$)

---

## ⚙️ 4. Step-by-Step Operating Procedure

### Phase A: Biomass Rehydration & Slurry Preparation
1. Charge the reactor with distilled/RO water to achieve a solid-to-liquid ratio of **$1:10\text{ (w/v)}$** ($100\text{g}$ dry algal powder per $1\text{L}$ water).
2. Engage the mechanical stirrer at $350\text{ RPM}$ and heat the slurry to $50^\circ\text{C}$.
3. Allow biomass to rehydrate for **45 minutes** under continuous agitation.

### Phase B: Thermochemical Alkaline Extraction
1. Slowly add Potassium Hydroxide ($\text{KOH}$) until reaching an operational concentration of $0.5M - 1.0M$ ($\text{pH} \approx 10.5 - 11.5$).
2. Elevate reactor temperature to **$85^\circ\text{C} - 90^\circ\text{C}$** and maintain under reflux for **2.5 hours**.
3. High-shear mixing breaks down cell walls, solubilizing structural polysaccharides into oligomers.

### Phase C: Neutralization, Filtration & Stabilization
1. Cool the hydrolysate to $40^\circ\text{C}$.
2. Slowly dose Phosphoric Acid ($\text{H}_3\text{PO}_4$) or Citric Acid under heavy agitation to adjust final pH to **$6.8 - 7.4$**.
3. Pass the raw extract through a $50\ \mu\text{m}$ filtration screen to remove insoluble cell debris.
4. Add $0.2\%\text{ w/v}$ Potassium Sorbate as a biocide/preservative and homogenize for 15 minutes.
