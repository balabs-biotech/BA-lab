## 🎯 1. Objective & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) details the industrial formulation protocol for loading porous pyrolyzed carbon (biochar) with MARINOR seaweed hydrolysate, humic substances, and viable endospores of *Bacillus spp.* (PGPR). The result is a biologically active, nutrient-dense soil amendment (Terra Preta Matrix) designed to increase soil Cation Exchange Capacity (CEC), improve Water Holding Capacity (WHC), and protect microbial inoculants against abiotically harsh soils.

---

## ⚠️ 2. Biosafety & PPE

* **Biosafety Grade:** BSL-1.
* **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):**
  * FFP3 respirator mask during biochar crushing, sieving, and dry blending.
  * Heavy-duty nitrile/rubber gloves and protective apron during hydrolysate liquid handling.
  * Safety goggles.
* **Dust Hazard Warning:** Fine biochar dust ($<100\ \mu\text{m}$) is explosible in high air concentrations and severe respiratory irritant. Perform crushing in ventilated areas or under active misting.

## 🔬 3. Raw Material Specifications & Input Ratio

To achieve optimal internal micro-pore saturation without structural collapse of the carbon matrix, the liquid-to-solid impregnation mass ratio ($R_{LS}$) is defined as:

$$R_{LS} = \frac{V_{\text{MARINOR}} + V_{\text{Inoculant}}}{M_{\text{Biochar}}} \approx 1.25 \quad (\text{L/kg})$$

## ⚙️ 4. Step-by-Step Production Protocol

```text
[ Dry Biochar (100kg) ] ──> [ Mechanical Milling <2mm ] ──> [ MARINOR Liquid Impregnation (100L Total) ] ──> [ Curing Phase 48h ] ──> [ Packaging ]

Phase A: Mechanical Preparation & Particle SizingProcess raw Kon-Tiki biochar through a hammer mill or rotary shredder.
Sieve the crushed carbon to retain the fraction between $0.5\ \text{mm}$ and $2.0\ \text{mm}$.
Reject fines ($<0.1\ \text{mm}$) to maintain aeration pathways in the finished granule.

Phase B: Liquid Loading & Vacuum ImpregnationTransfer $100\ \text{kg}$ of sieved biochar into a horizontal paddle mixer or modified concrete mixer drum.
In a secondary vessel, combine $75\ \text{L}$ MARINOR liquid, $5\ \text{kg}$ Potassium Humate, and $3\ \text{kg}$ Molasses in $20\ \text{L}$ deionized water.
Stir until fully dissolved.Start drum rotation ($25 - 30\ \text{RPM}$) and spray the MARINOR-humic mixture uniformly over the tumbling biochar using low-pressure nozzles ($2.0\ \text{bar}$).
Allow continuous tumbling for 45 minutes to ensure total capillary uptake into the carbon micropores.

Phase C: Biological Inoculation & StabilizationReduce drum rotation speed to $15\ \text{RPM}$.Slowly spray $25\ \text{L}$ of Bacillus spp.
 spore broth directly onto the impregnated biochar.Mix for an additional 15 minutes to distribute endospores evenly across the carbon surface.
Discharge the moist mixture into breathable curing vats. Allow to age for 48 hours at $22^\circ\text{C} - 28^\circ\text{C}$ for biological equilibration.
