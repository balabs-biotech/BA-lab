# 🧪 SOP-BAC-001: Axenic Isolation and Phenotypic Characterization of PGPR (Bacillus spp.)

* **Document Code:** SOP-BAC-001
* **Version:** 1.0
* **Biosafety Level:** BSL-1
* **Target Organisms:** Plant Growth-Promoting Rhizobacteria (*Bacillus subtilis*, *Bacillus amyloliquefaciens*)
* **Author:** B.A. LAB project — Applied Bacteriology Unit

---

## 🎯 1. Objective & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the isolation, axenic purification, and preliminary phenotypic screening of spore-forming Plant Growth-Promoting Rhizobacteria (PGPR) from rhizospheric soils. The objective is to select robust bacterial strains capable of solubilizing inorganic phosphate, synthesizing IAA (Indole-3-Acetic Acid), and producing antifungal lipopeptides for biostimulant formulation.

---

## ⚠️ 2. Biosafety & Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

* **Biosafety Grade:** BSL-1 (Non-pathogenic environmental isolates).
* **PPE Required:**
  * Cleanroom-grade lab coat and sterile powder-free nitrile gloves.
  * Safety goggles during thermal pasteurization and chemical handling.
  * FFP2 respirator mask when handling dry culture media powders.
* **Decontamination:** Autoclave all biohazard waste at $121^\circ\text{C}$ for 30 minutes prior to disposal. Sanitize workstations with $70\%$ ethanol before and after operations.

## ⚙️ 4. Step-by-Step Operating Procedure

### Phase A: Rhizospheric Sampling & Serial Dilution
1. Weigh $10\text{g}$ of active root-adjacent soil sample into $90\text{mL}$ of sterile $0.9\%\text{ NaCl}$ solution to form the initial suspension ($10^{-1}$).
2. Homogenize on an orbital shaker at $180\text{ RPM}$ for **25 minutes** at $25^\circ\text{C}$.
3. Prepare a ten-fold serial dilution series ($10^{-2}$ to $10^{-7}$) under the laminar flow hood using sterile micro-tubes.

### Phase B: Thermal Selection of Endospore Formers
1. Transfer $1\text{mL}$ from dilutions $10^{-3}$, $10^{-4}$, and $10^{-5}$ into sterile thin-walled PCR/micro-centrifuge tubes.
2. Heat-treat the samples in a water bath at **$80^\circ\text{C}$ for 20 minutes** to eliminate vegetative non-spore-forming cells while preserving *Bacillus* endospores.
3. Immediately cool tubes in an ice bath to $4^\circ\text{C}$ for 5 minutes.

### Phase C: Inoculation & Isolation
1. Spread $100\ \mu\text{L}$ of heat-treated suspension onto Tryptic Soy Agar (TSA) plates in triplicate.
2. Incubate inverted plates at $30^\circ\text{C} \pm 1^\circ\text{C}$ for **24 to 48 hours**.
3. Select colonies displaying typical *Bacillus* morphology (creamy-white, dry, irregular/rhizoid margins).
4. Perform streak-plating three consecutive times on fresh TSA to achieve axenic (pure) monocultures.
