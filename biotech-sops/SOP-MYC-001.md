# 🍄 SOP-MYC-001: Axenic Maintenance, Subculturing, and Long-Term Preservation of Elite Fungal Strains

* **Document Code:** SOP-MYC-001
* **Version:** 1.0
* **Biosafety Level:** BSL-1
* **Target Species:** *Hericium erinaceus*, *Pleurotus ostreatus*, *Cordyceps militaris*, *Agrocybe aegerita*
* **Author:** B.A. LAB project — Mycology Unit

---

## 🎯 1. Objective & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the protocol for maintaining vegetative mycelial vigor, avoiding somatic senescence (sectoring), and ensuring high-viability long-term storage of elite fungal cultures in the B.A. LAB project Strain Bank. It covers routine aseptic transfer, mineral oil overlay for medium-term preservation, and cryopreservation protocols.

---

## ⚠️ 2. Biosafety & PPE

* **Biosafety Level:** BSL-1.
* **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):**
  * Lab coat and sterile, powder-free nitrile gloves.
  * FFP2 mask when working with sporulating cultures (*Cordyceps*, mature *Pleurotus*).
  * Eye protection when handling liquid nitrogen or chemical sterilants.
* **Aseptic Standard:** All transfers must occur within a Class 100 / ISO 5 Laminar Flow Hood ($v = 0.45\ \text{m/s}$) after a 15-minute UV-C decontamination cycle.

## 🔬 3. Equipment & Reagents

### Equipment
* HEPA Class 100 Horizontal Laminar Flow Hood
* Flame/Induction Micro-sterilizer or Bunsen Burner
* Stainless Steel Scalpel (Blade #11) and Inoculation Loops
* Temperature-controlled Incubator ($23^\circ\text{C} - 25^\circ\text{C} \pm 0.5^\circ\text{C}$)

### Media & Preservatives
* Malt Extract Yeast Powder Agar (MYPA): $20\text{g/L}$ Malt Extract, $2\text{g/L}$ Yeast Extract, $15\text{g/L}$ Agar
* Potato Dextrose Yeast Agar (PDYA): Infusion from $200\text{g}$ potatoes, $20\text{g}$ Dextrose, $2\text{g}$ Yeast Extract, $15\text{g}$ Agar
* Sterile Heavy Mineral Oil (Autoclaved at $121^\circ\text{C}$ for 60 min, dry-heated at $160^\circ\text{C}$ for 2h)
* Cryoprotectant Solution: $15\%\text{ (v/v)}$ Glycerol + $5\%\text{ (w/v)}$ Trehalose in deionized water

---

## ⚙️ 4. Step-by-Step Operating Procedure

### Phase A: Aseptic Subculturing (Mother Culture to Working Plates)
1. Sanitize the laminar bench with $70\%$ ethanol and flame scalpel until red-hot. Allow blade to cool on sterile agar margin.
2. Excise a $5\text{mm} \times 5\text{mm}$ agar plug from the leading edge of an actively growing, non-sectored mycelial colony.
3. Transfer the plug, mycelium-side down, onto the center of a fresh MYPA or PDYA Petri dish.
4. Seal plates with Parafilm M and incubate inverted at **$24^\circ\text{C} \pm 1^\circ\text{C}$** in total darkness.
5. **Generational Limit:** Never exceed **P5** (Passage 5) from the Master Culture to prevent epigenetic degeneration.

### Phase B: Medium-Term Preservation (Mineral Oil Overlay, 12–24 Months)
1. Inoculate slant tubes containing MYPA medium with target mycelium.
2. Incubate at $24^\circ\text{C}$ until the mycelium covers $80\%$ of the slant surface.
3. Under aseptic conditions, pour sterile heavy mineral oil over the slant until the culture and agar are submerged by **at least $10\text{mm}$ of oil**.
4. Store upright at **$4^\circ\text{C} \pm 1^\circ\text{C}$** in darkness.

### Phase C: Cryopreservation (-80°C Deep Freezing)
1. Harvest $5\text{mm}$ mycelial plugs from the active margin of a P1/P2 culture.
2. Place 5 plugs into a sterile $2\text{mL}$ internal-thread cryovial containing $1.5\text{mL}$ of sterile Cryoprotectant Solution ($15\%$ Glycerol + $5\%$ Trehalose).
3. Equilibrium period: Allow plugs to soak at $4^\circ\text{C}$ for **30 minutes**.
4. Controlled Cooling: Decrease temperature at a rate of **$-1^\circ\text{C}/\text{min}$** using a isopropyl alcohol freezing container down to $-80^\circ\text{C}$.
