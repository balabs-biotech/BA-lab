# 🌿 SOP-VIT-001: Preparation of Stock Solutions and Murashige & Skoog (MS) Basal Medium Ex Nihilo for Micropropagation and TIS

* **Document Code:** SOP-VIT-001
* **Version:** 1.0
* **Biosafety Level:** BSL-1
* **Target Application:** Plant Tissue Culture, Organogenesis, and Temporary Immersion Systems (TIS / RITA / Twin-Bottle)
* **Author:** B.A. LAB project — Micropropagation & Plant Biotechnology Unit

---

## 🎯 1. Objective & Scope

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) details the formulation and preparation of full-strength Murashige & Skoog (MS, 1962) basal medium *ex nihilo* (from primary inorganic salts, vitamins, and organic supplements) without relying on commercial premixes. It guarantees batch-to-batch chemical reproducibility for micropropagation and liquid-phase Temporary Immersion Systems (TIS).

---

## ⚠️ 2. Safety, Biosafety & PPE

* **Biosafety Grade:** BSL-1.
* **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):**
  * Cleanroom lab coat and sterile powder-free nitrile gloves.
  * FFP2 dust mask when weighing anhydrous inorganic salts and plant growth regulators (PGRs).
  * Eye protection (goggles) during acid/base pH adjustments.
* **Chemical Hazards:** Concentrated stock solutions contain nitrates ($\text{NH}_4\text{NO}_3$, $\text{KNO}_3$) and heavy metal salts ($\text{CuSO}_4$, $\text{CoCl}_2$). Handle PGR powders (IBA, NAA, BAP) inside a fume hood or HEPA enclosure.

---

## 🔬 3. Stock Solution Formulation Matrix

To avoid micro-precipitation of phosphate and sulfate complexes during storage, four separate concentrated stock solutions must be prepared using ultrapure Type I water ($18.2\ \text{M}\Omega\cdot\text{cm}$).

### Stock A: Macronutrients ($10\times$ Concentration)
Dissolve reagents sequentially in $800\text{mL}$ $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ before bringing final volume to $1000\text{mL}$:
* Ammonium Nitrate ($\text{NH}_4\text{NO}_3$): $16.50\ \text{g/L}$
* Potassium Nitrate ($\text{KNO}_3$): $19.00\ \text{g/L}$
* Calcium Chloride Anhydrous ($\text{CaCl}_2$): $3.32\ \text{g/L}$ (or $\text{CaCl}_2\cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$: $4.40\ \text{g/L}$)
* Magnesium Sulfate Heptahydrate ($\text{MgSO}_4\cdot 7\text{H}_2\text{O}$): $3.70\ \text{g/L}$
* Potassium Phosphate Monobasic ($\text{KH}_2\text{PO}_4$): $1.70\ \text{g/L}$

### Stock B: Micronutrients ($100\times$ Concentration)
* Boric Acid ($\text{H}_3\text{BO}_3$): $620.0\ \text{mg/L}$
* Manganese Sulfate Monohydrate ($\text{MnSO}_4\cdot\text{H}_2\text{O}$): $1690.0\ \text{mg/L}$
* Zinc Sulfate Heptahydrate ($\text{ZnSO}_4\cdot 7\text{H}_2\text{O}$): $860.0\ \text{mg/L}$
* Potassium Iodide ($\text{KI}$): $83.0\ \text{mg/L}$
* Sodium Molybdate Dihydrate ($\text{Na}_2\text{MoO}_4\cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$): $25.0\ \text{mg/L}$
* Copper Sulfate Pentahydrate ($\text{CuSO}_4\cdot 5\text{H}_2\text{O}$): $2.5\ \text{mg/L}$
* Cobalt Chloride Hexahydrate ($\text{CoCl}_2\cdot 6\text{H}_2\text{O}$): $2.5\ \text{mg/L}$

### Stock C: Iron-Chelate ($100\times$ Concentration)
* Ferrous Sulfate Heptahydrate ($\text{FeSO}_4\cdot 7\text{H}_2\text{O}$): $2.78\ \text{g/L}$
* Disodium EDTA ($\text{Na}_2\text{EDTA}\cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$): $3.73\ \text{g/L}$
* *Procedure:* Heat solution to $60^\circ\text{C}$ under continuous stirring for 30 minutes until amber/golden color stabilizes. Store in a dark bottle to prevent photo-degradation.

### Stock D: Vitamins & Organics ($1000\times$ Concentration)
* Myo-Inositol: $100.0\ \text{g/L}$ (or added directly at $100\ \text{mg/L}$ per liter of final medium)
* Nicotinic Acid (Niacin): $0.50\ \text{g/L}$
* Pyridoxine HCl (Vitamin B6): $0.50\ \text{g/L}$
* Thiamine HCl (Vitamin B1): $0.10\ \text{g/L}$
* Glycine: $2.00\ \text{g/L}$

## ⚙️ 4. Step-by-Step Operating Procedure for 1 Litre Final Medium
[ 800mL H2O ] ──> [ Add Stocks A, B, C, D ] ──> [ Add Sucrose 30g ] ──> [ Adjust pH 5.8 ] ──> [ Autoclave 121°C/20min ]

Phase A: Assembly & Carbon SourcePour $800\text{mL}$ of distilled/deionized water into a $1000\text{mL}$ PYREX beaker equipped with a magnetic stir bar.Add the stock solutions using volumetric pipettes:
Stock A (Macros): $100.0\ \text{mL}$Stock B (Micros): $10.0\ \text{mL}$Stock C (Iron-EDTA): $10.0\ \text{mL}$Stock D (Vitamins): $1.0\ \text{mL}$Weigh and dissolve $30.0\text{g}$ of Plant-Grade Sucrose ($3\%\text{ w/v}$).
Phase B: PGR Addition & pH AdjustmentAdd target Plant Growth Regulators (e.g., $0.5\ \text{mg/L}$ BAP, $0.1\ \text{mg/L}$ IBA) from $1.0\ \text{mg/mL}$ stock solutions as required by the specific culture protocol.Adjust total volume to $950\text{mL}$ with deionized water.Place pH probe into the medium under active stirring. Adjust pH to $5.80 \pm 0.05$ using $0.1M\ \text{KOH}$ or $0.1M\ \text{HCl}$.Bring final volume strictly to $1000\text{mL}$.
Phase C: Gelling (For Solid Media) or TIS AllocationFor Solid Culture: Add $7.0\ \text{g/L}$ Plant Tissue Culture Grade Agar or $2.5\ \text{g/L}$ Phytagel. Heat mixture to $90^\circ\text{C}$ until gelling agent is completely clarified.For TIS Systems: Dispense liquid medium directly into Twin-Bottle or RITA vessels ($150 - 250\text{mL}$ per vessel).Sterilization: Autoclave at $121^\circ\text{C}$ ($15\text{ psi}$) for 20 minutes.
