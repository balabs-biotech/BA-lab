# 💧 Bioclimatic Condensation Collectors & Passive Water Management

Design principles for passive atmospheric water harvesting and microclimate humidity management in semi-arid environments.

## System Architecture
* **Lime-Marbled Condensation Surfaces:** Passive radiative cooling surfaces engineered to drop below dew point temperatures during nocturnal radiative cooling cycles.
* **Thermal Mass Stabilization:** Integrated water wall reservoirs and biochar-amended technosoils used to buffer diurnal temperature swings in the rooftop greenhouse.

## Microclimate Dynamics
$$\text{Nocturnal Cooling} \longrightarrow T_{\text{surface}} < T_{\text{dewpoint}} \longrightarrow \text{Condensation Runoff Collection}$$

* **Water Integration:** Harvested condensation runoff is filtered and channeled into low-pressure sub-surface irrigation loops for plant acclimatization modules.
