# 🌾 Agricultural Straw Shredder & Processing Unit

Specifications and modifications for the custom straw shredder used for fungal substrate particle sizing.

## Processing Requirements
* **Optimal Particle Size:** $2 - 5\text{ cm}$ (ideal for mycelial colonization density and gas exchange in substrate bags).
* **Throughput Target:** $>150\text{ kg/h}$ dry straw processing.

## Key Modifications
* **Blade Geometry:** Tempered steel cutting knives angled for clean shear rather than pulverization.
* **Acoustic Dampening:** Sound-insulated enclosure lining to minimize workshop noise levels during batch processing.
