# 🌀 Modified Mechanical Substrate Mixer

Technical documentation for the heavy-duty substrate blending unit used to homogenize substrate batches (straw, biochar, supplements, and hydration components).

## Mechanical Features
* **Drive Mechanism:** Modified geared electric motor ($220\text{V}$, reduced RPM for high torque).
* **Mixing Vessel:** Reinforced PEHD / steel drum.
* **Internal Paddles:** Custom helical mixing arms designed to distribute moisture evenly without tearing straw fibers.

## Operational Parameters
* **Batch Capacity:** $80 - 120\text{ L}$ per mixing cycle.
* **Mixing Time:** $10 - 15\text{ minutes}$ for full homogeneity.
* **Safety Integration:** Emergency stop loop and protective grid overlay.
